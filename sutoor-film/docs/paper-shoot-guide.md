# Filming the paper shots on an iPhone (about 15 minutes)

The film is already built around three paper scenes, so each clip you film drops straight into its place:
- the timing (when each verse is spoken);
- the subtitles above and below the strip;
- the closing glow on «النِصالِ»;
- the sound.

## You need
- **Three strips of plain off-white paper**, about 25 × 7 cm, cut with scissors. Printer paper works.
- **A black marker or a calligraphy pen.** Write one line per strip in your best hand, with the diacritics:
  1. يُدَفِّنُ بَعضُنا بَعضاً وَتَمشي
  2. وَهانَ فَما أُبالي بِالرَزايا
  3. تَكَسَّرَتِ النِصالُ عَلى النِصالِ
- **A lighter, a candle and a rug or carpet**, for the opening shot.
- **A stone or paved surface outdoors at night, and a bucket of water.** Never burn paper indoors or near anything that can catch fire.

## Shots
| # | Shot | How | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strip 1 held in your hand over the rug, a lit candle in the background | Phone in portrait, 30 cm away, still. Fill the width with the strip | 4 s |
| 2 | Strip 1 on the ground, burning | Lay the strip flat and light it at the middle of the bottom edge. Film straight down from 30–40 cm. Keep filming until half the strip has burned | 10 s |
| 3 | Strip 2 on the ground, burning slowly | Same as shot 2. Light one corner only | 10 s |
| 4 | Strip 3 on the ground, burning (the closing scene) | Same as shot 2. Light the middle of the bottom edge. Let the flames grow | 10 s |

## Phone settings
- 1080p or 4K at 30 fps, portrait, flash off.
- Tap on the paper to focus, then hold your finger to lock focus and exposure.
- Keep the phone still: rest your elbows on your knees.
